Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Diamond Athletics Holdings (operating as Diamond HQ) ("Diamond HQ", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information through Scorekeeper and Basepath (the "Services"). We are based in British Columbia, Canada, and follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored hashed by Firebase Authentication) and role (coach/manager or player).
  • Teams and rosters: team names, rosters, lineups, league and tournament membership entered by coaches and managers. When a player links their Basepath account to a roster spot, we connect that account to the team data.
  • Game data: scores, stats and related records entered in Scorekeeper. Personal stats you log in Basepath are currently stored on your device only and are not synced to our servers (stats synced through licensed leagues are stored with the league).
  • Check-in and calendar: availability and check-in responses; game and event locations from a shared list; calendar feed data if you use the calendar subscription feature.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a device token is stored on your account so we can send them.
  • Photos and logos: any you add are currently kept for your session only and are not saved on our servers.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation and status of payments and your Stripe customer reference, but not your full card number.
  • Technical: standard information such as IP address and browser or device details, collected automatically when you use the web app.

2. How we use it

To create and run your account; operate scoring, rosters, scheduling, check-in and league and tournament features; process payments; send transactional emails and notifications; keep the Services working and secure; contact you about your account or billing; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Your team or league: roster, lineup, scoring and check-in data is visible to the relevant coaches, managers and, in some cases, other players, as part of how the Services work.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, functions, messaging), Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional email) and GitHub Pages (hosting the web app). They process data for us under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal: where required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, us or others.
  • Business changes: if we reorganize, merge or sell the business, information may transfer as part of that, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

4. Where data is stored

Our providers may store and process data outside Canada, in places whose privacy laws can differ from Canada's. By using the Services you accept that your information may be transferred there.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information while your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. If you delete your account we will delete or anonymize your information within a reasonable time, except what we must keep for legal, tax or accounting reasons (such as payment records).

6. Your rights

Under Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent for certain uses, and ask us to delete your account and information, subject to the retention exceptions above. Contact hello@diamondhq.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access rules in our database and standard authentication practices. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and local storage

The Services use browser local storage and similar technology to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

The Services are for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will tell you about material changes in the Services or by email where reasonable.
